# 📖 About Zenith

Zenith is an intelligent study management platform that combines **Artificial Intelligence**, **productivity systems**, and **learning science** to help students build better study habits and improve long-term retention.

Unlike traditional task managers or study planners, Zenith creates a complete learning ecosystem by integrating:

* AI-assisted tutoring
* Spaced repetition
* Focus systems
* Progress analytics
* Social learning
* Gamification

The goal is simple:

> Help students study efficiently, stay motivated, and understand concepts deeply instead of memorizing temporarily.

---

# ✨ Features

## 📚 Smart Syllabus Management

Students can add their complete syllabus and track progress by marking topics as completed.

Zenith automatically schedules revision sessions using spaced repetition intervals:

✅ Day 1
✅ Day 3
✅ Day 5
✅ Day 7
✅ Day 15
✅ Day 30

This reinforces concepts at optimized intervals and improves long-term memory retention.

---

## 📊 Dashboard & Study Analytics

Track study consistency and progress using visual insights.

Features:

* Daily revision reminders
* Topic completion tracking
* Study statistics
* Progress monitoring
* GitHub-style study heatmap

The study heatmap gradually darkens as study hours increase, helping students visualize consistency over time.

---

## ⏳ Productivity System

Built-in focus tools help students maintain concentration without burnout.

### Pomodoro Modes

🍅 50 / 10

🍅 120 / 30

🍅 15 / 3

Additional tools:

* Custom timer
* Stopwatch

---

## 🤖 AI Tutor

Zenith includes multiple AI tutoring modes tailored to different learning styles.

### Beginner Mode

* Explains concepts from fundamentals
* Ideal for first-time learners

### Moderate Mode

* Standard explanation mode
* Balanced teaching approach

### Hard Mode

* Gives hints instead of direct answers
* Encourages critical thinking

### Quiz Mode

* Interactive questions
* Reinforces concepts using active recall
